Why Flood Insurance Matters

Standard home and renters policies don’t cover flood. Even “not in a flood zone” doesn’t mean “no risk” — it often just means the mortgage company doesn’t require it. One bad spring and you could be staring at foundation, flooring, and contents damage.

Sometimes private carriers can offer better limits, faster underwriting, or lower deductibles — I’ll quote both so you can compare.
