Why I do this

Insurance can feel confusing, bossy, or just… exhausting. Most folks don’t want 20 pages of jargon — they want to know “Am I covered?” and “Can I afford it?” That’s where I come in.

I started JBIS to bring more integrity and options into the process. As an independent broker I can actually shop multiple carriers, explain the differences in plain language, and recommend the path I’d take myself.
