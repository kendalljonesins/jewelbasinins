Hi, I’m Kendall Jones.

I run Jewel Basin Insurance Solutions LLC because I believe people deserve options, straight answers, and someone who actually cares about their life — not just the product they try to fit you into.

Why I do this

Insurance can feel confusing, bossy, or just… exhausting. I’ve worked with enough people to know that most of the time, folks don’t actually want 20 pages of legal jargon — they want to know: “Am I covered?” and “Can I afford it?” That’s where I come in.

I started JBIS to bring more integrity and options into the process. I’m independent, which means I can actually shop around for you. If something doesn’t make sense, I’ll explain it. If I wouldn’t buy it myself, I won’t recommend it to you. No pushing you into a box you don't even want.

Beyond insurance

I’m not just a broker — I cook, I sing, I game, I write, and I have an ongoing love affair with being creative in the kitchen, no matter how chaotic it may get at times.

When I’m not working with clients, I’m usually:

I care a lot because I’ve had to rebuild, pivot, and stand up for myself more than once. Helping other people protect what they love is part business, part calling.
