About Me

Local service, real options, and coverage designed around people — not quotas.

Hi, I’m Kendall Jones, owner and licensed broker at Jewel Basin Insurance Solutions LLC. I founded this agency with one goal — to simplify the insurance experience while keeping it honest, transparent, and personal.

I specialize in helping individuals, families, and small businesses find the right balance of protection and price. As an independent broker, I’m not tied to one company — I shop multiple carriers to find coverage that truly fits you.

I offer a wide range of insurance services, including:

  • Home, Auto, and Renters Insurance
  • Life Insurance & Mortgage Protection
  • Commercial Lines for small businesses and contractors
  • Voluntary benefits through Aflac
  • Telehealth partnerships through Ally Health
  • Pet Insurance — because family includes fur, too

I believe insurance should feel empowering, not confusing. My mission is to change the way people view coverage — not as a burden, but as a shield that lets you live your life with confidence.
