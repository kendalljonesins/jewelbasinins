6. Washington Residents – Personal Injury Protection (PIP)

In accordance with Washington state law, all licensed agents must offer Personal Injury Protection (PIP) coverage when quoting automobile insurance. PIP provides no-fault benefits for medical expenses, lost wages, and funeral costs following a covered accident, regardless of fault.

  • Standard Limits: $10,000 for medical expenses, up to $200 per week for income loss (capped at $10,000 total), and up to $5,000 for funeral expenses.
  • Who Is Covered: Policyholders, household members, step/foster children, and non-family passengers or pedestrians injured in the accident.
  • Filing Deadline: PIP claims must be submitted within three (3) years of the accident date.
  • Exclusions: Injuries sustained during felonies, racing, or operation of excluded vehicles such as motorcycles, mopeds, or recreational vehicles.
  • Waiver Option: Clients may choose to decline PIP coverage in writing. JBIS will document acceptance or waiver before binding coverage.
