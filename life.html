What I can help you with

Every situation is different — that’s why I shop multiple carriers instead of forcing you into one box.

Mortgage Protection

Keep the roof over your family’s head. If you pass away during the covered period, your mortgage is protected so your loved ones aren’t left with the payment.

  • Term plans aligned to your loan
  • Can include living benefits (depending on carrier)
  • Great for first-time homeowners

Term, Whole, & Universal

Options for short-term, long-term, or lifelong coverage — we’ll look at rates side-by-side so you know what you’re paying for.

  • Affordable term for income replacement
  • Whole life for cash value & legacy
  • Universal for flexible premiums

Final Expense & Burial

Smaller policies meant to take care of funeral, travel, and last bills — so your family can focus on healing, not invoices.

  • Lenient underwriting (some health issues ok)
  • Fast-issue carriers available
  • Great for parents & grandparents

Income Replacement

Protect the paycheck your household runs on. If something happens to you, your family can still cover bills, groceries, and day-to-day life.

  • Set benefit amounts
  • Can be paired with mortgage protection
  • Perfect for single-income homes
